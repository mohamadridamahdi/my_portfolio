// about

I'm a full-stack developer and Computer Science student at the Lebanese University. I like owning a product from start to finish: a clean, responsive interface on the front, and a secure, well-structured back end behind it.

Most of my time goes into DivoTech, the company I founded. It runs a multi-tenant SaaS platform where businesses edit their own website content from one dashboard, and every change publishes itself through a Git-based workflow. More than five businesses pay for it every month. I build it with AI-assisted development in Claude Code, which lets me go from requirements to production quickly while I stay in charge of the architecture.

Alongside that, I'm a Data Science Intern at AIAEC, where I collect and validate building codes and zoning data for US cities that feed ChatAEC, the company's AI product. I also trained in cybersecurity through Cisco Networking Academy, which shapes how I think about authentication and keeping each client's data separate.

I'm curious by nature. I don't like running code I don't understand, so I always dig into how each piece actually works.
